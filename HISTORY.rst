=======
History
=======


0.2.0 (2026-10-26)
------------------

* Added the thermal module (``thermal.py``) for module temperature and energy
  yield calculations for PV modules with and without sub-bandgap reflectors,
  as used in "How do sub-bandgap reflectors affect the performance of PV
  modules?" (arXiv:2604.20757).
* Added examples and Jupyter notebooks for the thermal module in
  ``examples/thermal``, including the analysis of the effect of sub-bandgap
  reflectors on module degradation and cumulative energy yield.
